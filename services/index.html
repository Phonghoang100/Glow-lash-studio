Services

Lash & brow services, explained honestly

Every service below begins with a consultation and ends with lashes your natural lashes can carry. Prices are starting points — see the full pricing page for fills, bundles, and add-ons, or book online at our Lawrenceville or Duluth studio.

Classic

Classic Set

One extension placed on one natural lash, across the entire lash line. Classic is the quietest set we offer — added length and definition that reads as your lashes on a very good day, not as lash extensions.

It suits first-time clients, minimal-makeup routines, and anyone whose natural lashes are already reasonably full. If you want texture or visible fullness, consider a hybrid set instead.

Duration about 2 hours · Lasts 2–3 weeks between fills · From $150 full set, $70 fill

Book Classic

Hybrid

Hybrid Set

A mapped blend of classic single extensions and lightweight volume fans, placed strategically to create texture and dimension. Hybrid is the set most clients in Lawrenceville and Duluth end up choosing after consultation — fuller than classic, softer than volume.

It flatters hooded and downturned eyes especially well, since the mix lets your artist build lift exactly where your eye shape needs it. See finished hybrid work in the gallery.

Duration about 2–2.5 hours · Lasts 2–3 weeks between fills · From $185 full set, $85 fill

Book Hybrid

Volume

Volume Set

Handmade fans of two to six ultra-fine extensions, each fan lighter than a single classic lash. Volume delivers visible fullness — a soft, dark lash line that holds up in photographs and replaces mascara, eyeliner, and often eyeshadow.

Because the fans are made by hand at the bed rather than pre-made, density goes precisely where your mapping calls for it. Ideal for sparse natural lashes and for clients who want unmistakable fullness that still moves naturally.

Duration about 2.5 hours · Lasts 2–3 weeks between fills · From $220 full set, $95 fill

Book Volume

Mega Volume

Mega Volume Set

Fans of ten or more extensions at 0.03mm — the finest fibers made — for maximum density with a surprisingly light footprint. Mega volume is a dramatic, editorial lash line: full, dark, and deliberate.

This set demands healthy natural lashes and an experienced hand, which is why we assess candidacy at consultation rather than by request. If your lashes need conditioning first, we'll build a plan to get you there.

Duration about 3 hours · Lasts 2–3 weeks between fills · From $260 full set, $110 fill

Book Mega Volume

Lifts & Tints

Lash Lift & Tint

A lash lift curls your own natural lashes from the root, holding for six to eight weeks. Add a tint and the effect is a clean, mascara-free lash line with zero maintenance beyond keeping them dry for the first day.

Lifts suit clients who want lift without extensions, are taking a break between sets, or simply have naturally long lashes that point straight out. A standalone tint is also available for lashes that curl well but run light in color.

Duration about 1 hour · Lasts 6–8 weeks · Lash Lift $95 · Lift + Tint $115 · Lash Tint $35

Brows

Brow Lamination, Shaping & Tint

Lamination sets brow hairs in a full, brushed-up direction that holds for weeks — the groomed-but-effortless brow you see in editorials. Shaping refines the arch to your bone structure, and tint adds depth that makes sparse areas read fuller.

Most clients book the three together: our lamination, shape, and tint bundle is $130 and pairs naturally with any lash appointment. Individually, lamination is $90, shaping $30, and tint $30 — all listed on the pricing page.

Duration 30–60 minutes · Lamination lasts 4–6 weeks · From $30

Removal

Gentle Removal

Extensions should never be picked, pulled, or grown out unevenly. Our removal uses a cream dissolver that releases the adhesive without stressing your natural lashes, followed by a cleanse and conditioning. It takes about thirty minutes and costs $40 — free when you're removing an outside studio's work to start a new set with us.

If you're switching from another studio in Gwinnett County, this is the right first appointment. Your artist will assess your natural lashes before mapping anything new.

Aftercare

How do you make a lash set last?

Keep new extensions dry for the first 24 hours while the adhesive fully cures. After that, wash them — daily, with an oil-free lash cleanser and a soft brush. Clean lashes retain dramatically better than babied ones, which is the most counterintuitive thing we teach new clients.

Avoid oil-based products near the eyes, skip waterproof mascara entirely, sleep on your back or a silk pillowcase if you can, and brush your lashes each morning with the spoolie from your kit. Every new-client set includes our aftercare kit ($38 on its own) with cleanser, brushes, and written instructions.

If retention ever seems short, mention it at your fill. Adhesive, aftercare, and even seasonal humidity all play a part, and small adjustments usually solve it. More detail lives in our FAQ and the journal.
