# Glow Lash Studio — Brand Identity

## Brand essence
Glow Lash Studio. The brand promises quiet luxury: precision lash artistry delivered in a calm, private, atelier-style studio. Not loud glamour — considered, editorial beauty for clients who invest in themselves.

**Tagline:** *Wake up beautiful.*
**Secondary line:** Luxury lash artistry in Lawrenceville & Duluth, Georgia.

## Voice
Calm, assured, editorial. Short sentences. No exclamation points. Speaks to an affluent client who values time, privacy, and craft. Never discounts-first; value is expressed through expertise, hygiene standards, and retention quality.

## Typography
- **Display/serif:** Cormorant Garamond (500/600, italic for accents) — headlines, prices, pull quotes.
- **Sans:** Jost (300/400/500) — body, UI, nav, buttons. Letter-spaced uppercase (0.12em) for eyebrows/labels/buttons.
- Scale: clamp-based; h1 ~ clamp(2.4rem, 6vw, 4.2rem).

## Art direction (placeholder imagery)

## Motion
