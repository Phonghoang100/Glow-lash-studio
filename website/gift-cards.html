Gift Cards

The gift of waking up beautiful

A digital gift card to Glow gives someone two unhurried hours in a quiet studio and weeks of effortless mornings after. Redeemable at both our Lawrenceville and Duluth studios.

How It Works

Delivered in minutes, welcome for years

Choose an amount from $50 to $500, add a note, and we email a beautifully designed digital card to your recipient — immediately, or scheduled for the morning of their birthday, anniversary, or wedding day. The card carries a unique code they present at checkout, and any balance stays on the card for next time.

Gift cards apply to every service on our menu and to retail, including aftercare kits. If your recipient is new to extensions, their first visit includes a consultation, so the gift works even when you are not sure which set suits them.

Suggested Amounts

Pairings clients love to give

Any amount works, but these three map to the experiences most often gifted.

The First Set

Covers a full Classic Set for someone who has always been curious — consultation and aftercare kit included. The most-given card, and the one that creates regulars.

Lift + Tint

Or choose your own amount

Delivered with the card, in your words.

The fine print, kept short

Glow gift cards never expire, in keeping with Georgia law, and there are no dormancy or maintenance fees — the amount you give is the amount they spend, whether that is next week or next year. Cards are redeemable for services and retail at either studio but are not redeemable or refundable for cash, and purchases are non-refundable. If a digital card email goes astray, contact us and we will reissue the code to the correct address. Gift cards may be applied toward a first month of Glow Circle membership, though ongoing membership billing requires a card on file. Standard studio policies, including cancellation notice, apply to appointments booked with gift cards.

Giving to someone nearby?

Both studios serve all of Gwinnett County — see our service areas to reassure your recipient the drive is short.
