Contact & Locations

Two studios, one standard

Historic downtown Lawrenceville and the Peachtree Industrial corridor in Duluth. Choose whichever is closer — pricing, artists' training, and hygiene protocols are identical.

Parking & arrival. We are one block from the Gwinnett Historic Courthouse on the Lawrenceville square. Free public parking sits behind the building off Luckie Street, with two-hour street spots on Crogan. Take the stairs or elevator to Suite 210 and have a seat in the lounge — your artist will come to you.
