The Solène Circle

For lashes that are never between fills

Membership turns lash care from a series of appointments into a standing ritual — one predictable monthly rate, priority on the book, and a set that never has an off week.

Gold

The essential rhythm

$129 / month

  • One fill monthly — any set level
  • 10% off additional services
  • Priority booking
  • Birthday lash bath + gift

For the client on a steady three-to-four-week fill cadence. A Volume fill alone runs $95 — Gold covers it and earns the rest back in member pricing.

Platinum

The complete ritual

$199 / month

  • One fill + one lift or brow service monthly
  • 15% off retail & additional services
  • 2 guest passes per year
  • First access to new artists' books

For the client who maintains lashes and brows together. The monthly lift-or-brow credit alone is worth up to $95 on our regular menu.

Join Platinum

Loyalty

Every visit counts, member or not

Every dollar you spend at Solène earns one loyalty point, and 250 points converts to a $25 credit toward any service or retail purchase. Points accrue automatically on your client profile — no punch card to lose, nothing to remember. A client on Volume fills every three weeks earns roughly $65 in credit over a year without changing a thing.

Give $25, get $25

When a friend books their first appointment through your referral link, they receive $25 off their new set and a $25 credit lands on your account after their visit. Your link lives in your booking profile and in every appointment confirmation email. There is no cap — several clients in Lawrenceville and Duluth have effectively covered a month of membership through referrals alone.

Membership terms

The Solène Circle carries a three-month minimum, after which you may cancel anytime with notice before your next billing date — no cancellation fee, no awkward phone call required. Monthly benefits are meant to keep a live set healthy on a real fill cadence, so they do not roll over; an unused fill in March does not become two fills in April. Benefits are personal to the member, with the exception of Platinum guest passes, which are yours to give. Membership pricing applies at both studios interchangeably, and standard studio policies on cancellation and late arrival apply to member appointments as they do to all others. Questions before joining? Ask us, or raise it at your next fill.

Not sure which tier fits?

Bring it up at any appointment and your artist will do the math against your actual fill history. New to Solène? Start with a set — membership can begin at your first fill.
