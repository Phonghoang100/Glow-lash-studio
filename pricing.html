Before You Book

What should you know about fills and deposits?

Fill eligibility. Fills require at least 40% of your extensions remaining. Below that, the honest and better-looking option is a new full set, and your artist will tell you which you need rather than guessing at the register. Good aftercare keeps most clients comfortably above the line.

New-client inclusions. Every new-client set includes a full consultation and our $38 aftercare kit at no additional cost.

Deposits. A $30 deposit reserves your appointment and is applied in full to your service. Rescheduling with 24 hours' notice moves the deposit with you — details are in our studio policies.

Outside work. Removal of another studio's extensions is free when you book a new set with us the same day.
