Lash extensions in Johns Creek and nearby communities

Nora Beauty Lounge is located on Medlock Bridge Road in Johns Creek, Georgia, and welcomes clients from across the surrounding area.

Whether you live in Johns Creek or drive in from a neighboring city, every appointment follows the same standards: a mapped consultation for new sets, careful application, and strict sanitation. Our online booking shows current availability, and you can compare services and pricing before you choose.

Johns Creek, GA

Our lounge on Medlock Bridge Road

You'll find us at 11600 Medlock Bridge Rd, Suite 130, Johns Creek, GA 30097. The lounge offers classic, hybrid, volume, and mega volume lash extensions, plus lash lifts and brow services.

Fills are recommended every two to three weeks. Directions, hours, and contact details are on our contact page.
