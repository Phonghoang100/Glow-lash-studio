Service Areas

Lash artistry for Gwinnett County and beyond

Two studios — historic Lawrenceville and the Duluth Peachtree Industrial corridor — placed so that most of Gwinnett County is within a 20-minute drive of a Solène chair.

Clients often ask which studio they should book. The honest answer is that it does not matter for the result — both locations share the same pricing, the same training standards, and the same hygiene protocols, and many clients float between the two depending on the day. What follows is a practical guide to which door is closer, wherever in the county you start from. When in doubt, our online booking shows real-time availability at both.

Lawrenceville, GA

The original studio, on the square

Our Lawrenceville studio sits one block from the Gwinnett Historic Courthouse, in the walkable heart of downtown. If you live near the square, in Sugarloaf-area neighborhoods, or along the 316 and Highway 20 corridors, this is your studio. Free public parking off Luckie Street means you can arrive five minutes before your appointment without circling the block.

Many clients pair a fill with lunch downtown or an evening at the Aurora Theatre — a two-to-three-week fill takes about an hour, which fits neatly before dinner reservations. Find the full address, hours, and arrival notes on our contact page.

Duluth, GA

The westside studio, near the Town Green

Our Duluth studio is on Peachtree Industrial Boulevard, a few minutes from the downtown Duluth Town Green and its restaurant row. It is the natural choice if you live or work along Pleasant Hill Road, near Gas South District, or on the western edge of the county toward Berkeley Lake. Front-lot parking sits steps from the Suite 140 entrance.

Weekday clients often book the 9am opening slot and are at their desks in Peachtree Corners or Norcross by late morning. Evening appointments pair well with dinner on the Town Green — Fridays we take our last full set at 4:30pm so no one is rushed.

Also Serving

Neighboring cities we see every week

These aren't abstract map circles — they are where our standing appointments actually drive in from.

Suwanee & Sugar Hill

About 15 minutes to Duluth down Peachtree Industrial, or an easy run to Lawrenceville via Lawrenceville-Suwanee Road. Clients near Town Center Park usually choose Duluth; those closer to Peachtree Industrial's eastern side split evenly.

Buford & Dacula

From the Mall of Georgia area, both studios are roughly 20 minutes — Duluth via I-85, Lawrenceville via GA-20. Dacula clients are closest to Lawrenceville, about 15 minutes along Winder Highway.

Snellville & Grayson

Lawrenceville is your studio — 15 minutes up Highway 124 from Snellville, closer still from Grayson. Saturday mornings are popular with this side of the county, so book a week or two ahead.

Johns Creek

Cross the river at State Bridge or Abbotts Bridge and you are at the Duluth studio in about 15 minutes. A number of our longest-standing volume clients make this drive every three weeks.

Norcross & Peachtree Corners

Duluth is 10 to 15 minutes up Peachtree Industrial from the Forum and historic Norcross. Lunchtime lash lifts are feasible here — a lift and tint runs about an hour.

Elsewhere in Gwinnett

Lilburn, Berkeley Lake, Braselton, and the Hamilton Mill area all reach one of our chairs inside 25 minutes. If you are choosing between studios, write to us and we will suggest the easier drive for your schedule.
