The Atelier

What makes an atelier different from a lash bar?

Most lash studios are built for throughput. Ours are built for the ninety minutes you spend on the bed. Each appointment is private — one artist, one client, one quiet room. No overlapping conversations, no rushing to the next chair.

Our artists train continuously, our adhesives are matched to the day's humidity, and every set is mapped to your eye shape before we begin. The result is work that photographs well on day one and still looks intentional in week three.

Meet the Atelier

Why Clients Trust Solène

Craft you can verify, not just admire

Founder Solène Marchand has spent eleven years behind the lash bed, training in Paris and Atlanta and holding both NovaLash and Borboleta certifications. Every artist on our team completes her internal certification before taking clients, and continues supervised education after — you can read each artist's background on our about page.

Hygiene is not a marketing line here. Tools are sterilized between every client, disposables are single-use without exception, and lash beds are stripped and sanitized after each appointment. We follow hospital-grade sanitation protocols because your eyes deserve nothing less careful.

And we stand behind retention. If you lose more than a reasonable share of your set in the first seventy-two hours despite following aftercare guidance, we make it right at no charge. Clients from Lawrenceville, Duluth, Suwanee, and Johns Creek drive past a dozen lash studios to reach us — that only works if the lashes last.

Good to Know

Questions we hear most

How long do lash extensions last?

A full set grows out with your natural lash cycle, so most clients book a fill every two to three weeks. With good aftercare — covered in detail on our services page — sets stay full and even between visits.

Do extensions damage your natural lashes?

Not when they're applied correctly. Damage comes from oversized extensions and rushed isolation, which is why we weigh every set against your natural lash health during consultation. Healthy lashes are the whole business model.

What should I do before my first appointment?

Arrive with clean, makeup-free lashes, skip your usual coffee if you can, and give yourself ten extra minutes. Full prep notes are on the booking page, and our FAQ covers everything else.
