The Lounge

What makes a lash lounge different from a lash bar?

Most lash bars are built for throughput. Ours is built for the ninety minutes you spend on the bed. Each appointment is private, with one artist, one client, and one quiet room. No overlapping conversations, no rushing to the next chair.

Our artists train continuously, our adhesives are matched to the day's humidity, and every set is mapped to your eye shape before we begin. The result is work that photographs well on day one and still looks intentional in week three.

Good to Know

Questions we hear most

How long do lash extensions last?

A full set grows out with your natural lash cycle, so most clients book a fill every two to three weeks. With good aftercare, covered in detail on our services page, sets stay full and even between visits.

Do extensions damage your natural lashes?

Not when they're applied correctly. Damage comes from oversized extensions and rushed isolation, which is why we weigh every set against your natural lash health during consultation. Healthy lashes are the whole business model.

What should I do before my first appointment?

Arrive with clean, makeup-free lashes, skip your usual coffee if you can, and give yourself ten extra minutes. Full prep notes are on the booking page, and our FAQ covers everything else.
