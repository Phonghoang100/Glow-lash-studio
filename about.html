Our Story

The lounge behind the lashes

Nora Beauty Lounge is a lash extension studio in Johns Creek, Georgia, built around careful work, private appointments, and lashes that look like yours, only better. Here is what we offer and how every visit works.

The Lounge

Lash extensions in Johns Creek, done with care

Plenty of lash bars are built for speed. Nora Beauty Lounge was built for the opposite: unhurried appointments, precise application, and honest guidance about the health of your natural lashes.

Clients visit us from across Johns Creek and nearby Alpharetta, Milton, Duluth, Suwanee, and Cumming. Whether it's your first set or your next fill, every visit is built around what you want and what your lashes can comfortably support.

Our Services

What can you book at our Johns Creek lounge?

Our menu focuses on lashes and brows, so every service gets our full attention.

Classic and hybrid lash extensions. Classic and hybrid sets are precise and natural, ideal if you want no one to guess you're wearing extensions.

Volume and mega volume lash extensions. Our volume and mega volume sets use hand-made fans as light as 0.03mm for fullness without unnecessary weight.

Lash lifts and brow services. From lash lifts to brow lamination and tinting, these services enhance what you already have, between sets or in place of extensions.

Every artist completes our internal certification, a standard stricter than any state requirement, before taking a single client. You'll find full details on our services and pricing pages.

Philosophy

Why does every new set start with a consultation?

Because the right set for you isn't always the fullest one. Your eye shape, natural lash strength, daily routine, and even how you sleep all affect what will look good and hold up well. Every new client starts with a mapped consultation, included with the set, so we can choose curl, length, and density together.

We would rather apply a classic set that suits you for years than a mega volume set your natural lashes can't support. The result is polished, wearable beauty instead of heavy glamour. It's also why our fill clients return every two to three weeks instead of starting over every two months.

Hygiene & Sanitation

What are our sanitation standards?

That same care extends to cleanliness. Everything that touches your eyes is either sterilized or single-use. Tweezers and other reusable tools go through a medical-grade autoclave between clients. Lash brushes, under-eye pads, adhesive wells, and applicators are thrown away after one use, every time.

Each lash bed is stripped, sanitized, and re-linened after every appointment, and HEPA filtration keeps adhesive fumes from lingering in the lounge. Our artists wash and glove between clients and complete annual bloodborne-pathogen and sanitation training. If you have sensitivities or a history of reactions, let us know when you book. We offer sensitive adhesives and patch testing, and our FAQ explains what to expect.

What We Hold To

Our values, in practice

These principles guide every appointment at the lounge.

Craft over speed. Appointments are scheduled with room to breathe. Our artists are never measured by how many clients they see in a day.

Honesty about lash health. If your natural lashes need a break, we'll tell you and suggest a lift or a rest instead of another set.

Privacy. One artist, one client, one room. Your appointment is your time.

Consistency. Whichever artist you book with, the mapping standards, products, and sanitation protocols are the same.
