Our Story

The lounge behind the lashes

Nora Beauty Lounge in Johns Creek, Georgia was built on a simple conviction: lash artistry deserves the same care, privacy, and craft as any other fine service. Here is who we are and how we work.

The Founder

Ava Monroe

Ava began her training in Paris, where lash work was treated as an apprenticeship rather than a certificate course, with months of mapping, isolation drills, and adhesive chemistry before a paying client ever sat in the chair. She carried that discipline to Atlanta, refined it over eleven years of practice, and earned certifications from both NovaLash and Borboleta along the way.

She opened Nora Beauty Lounge in Johns Creek because she saw a gap in the area: plenty of fast lash bars, very few places where the work was slow, precise, and honest about lash health. Clients from Alpharetta, Milton, Duluth, Suwanee, and Cumming now make the drive to find it.

Ava still takes clients weekly, and she personally trains and certifies every artist who joins the team. You can see her recent work in the gallery or request her directly when you book online.

Philosophy

Why do we insist on consultation first?

Because the best set for you is not the biggest set we can sell you. Eye shape, natural lash strength, lifestyle, even how you sleep. All of it changes what will actually look good and hold well. Every new client starts with a mapped consultation, included with the set, before we choose curl, length, or density together.

We would rather place a classic set that flatters you for years than a mega volume set your natural lashes can't carry. That restraint is the whole point of a lash lounge: considered, editorial beauty rather than loud glamour. It's also why our fill clients, detailed on the pricing page, return every two to three weeks instead of starting over every two months.

Hygiene & Sanitation

What are our sanitation standards?

Everything that touches your eyes is either sterilized or single-use. Tweezers and reusable tools go through a medical-grade autoclave between clients, not a quick wipe-down. Lash brushes, under-eye pads, adhesive wells, and applicators are disposed of after one use, every time.

Beds are stripped, sanitized, and re-linened after each appointment, and the lounge runs HEPA filtration so adhesive fumes never linger. Artists wash and glove between clients and complete annual bloodborne-pathogen and sanitation training. If you have sensitivities or a history of reactions, tell us when you book. We offer sensitive adhesives and patch testing, and our FAQ covers what to expect.

What We Hold To

Our values, in practice

Craft over speed. Appointments are booked with room to breathe. No artist here is measured by how many clients she can turn in a day.

Honesty about lash health. If your natural lashes need a break, we'll say so and suggest a lift or a rest instead of another set.

Privacy. One artist, one client, one room. Your appointment is your hour.

Consistency. Whether you book with Ava or any artist, the mapping standards, products, and sanitation protocols are identical.
